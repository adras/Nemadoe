Why
we
built
this.

Every editor wants your files in their cloud. Every app wants an account. Every subscription wants your credit card. We got tired of it.

NEMADEO is the editor we wanted to exist. A single file. Drop it anywhere, open it, write. Close it, your file is on your disk — not on someone else's server.

No sync to worry about. No breach to fear. No terms of service that change quietly in the night. Just you, a browser, and your words.
